## Production acceptance gates

### OPEN — opening pursuit, `ZS-S001.P01`
- One single coherent moment on a rain-slick elevated megacity roadway, 1980s–1990s-inspired cel-anime characters, painterly cyberpunk environment.
- Two **distinct squad cars in motion**, visually separable from Karl's vehicle: **Alex drives lead car; healthy Misaki beside him. Pete drives following car; Jacque beside him**.
- For crop viability, faces/wardrobe through both cabin windows should be discernible enough that lead cabin and following cabin may be cropped as unchanged subsets of the **same** original moment.
- Camera: external oblique view; preserve consistent traffic geography and squad relative position. No four-person single cabin, no transfer already performed, no additional unnamed squad members.
- No UI boards, spec sheets, labels, caption boxes, typography or misleading road signs baked into image. Keep quiet lower-right for HTML provisional caption.
- Check original-source match against accepted provisional character refs; any unresolved likeness differences must be noted for owner judgment.

### FORK — Midtown transfer bay, `ZS-S001.P05`
- A **different original single scene**, two cars **stopped** in a plausible transfer bay just before divergent routes toward Downtown CBD and Uptown/Galaxical Tower; physical doors/space between cars support a subsequent exchange.
- **Before** the fork choice: Alex/Misaki still occupy lead car; Pete/Jacque still in following car. No exchanged crew composition depicted until a route is chosen.
- Composition must support a **ramp-only crop with stopped cars excluded** and a lead-cabin decision detail. No invented directional text; map/navigation guidance stays HTML.
- Suspended urgency, rain, clear spatial separation of stopped bay and two diverging roads, consistent with the OPEN city and vehicles.
- No UI, storyboards, montage, multi-panels, baked words, posters or explanatory infographics.

### Asset quality and portability
- Prefer 2560×1440 original landscape; 1280×720 is acceptable with less aggressive crops. WebP for delivery, retain lossless approved master if available.
- $0 incremental cost; use only tools already available at no incremental charge. No unapproved paid subscriptions, providers or bulk art generation.
- Document generation/source method, whether model-generated, creator rights/terms as actually evidenced, SHA-256, original dimensions, output format, and image provenance. No unsupported claim of perpetual/unrestricted rights.
- Review crop rectangles on **actual image pixels**, not invented ROIs. If identities are too small, prefer new composition/looser crop or text-led fallback over unsafe upscale.
- Provide rendered side-by-side owner comparisons with approved old presentation; keep original files local and Git-tracked, no external URL dependency.
- Only after explicit **owner art approval** may images be activated as new live panel bindings; review of a plan does not constitute image approval.
