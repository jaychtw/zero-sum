- Cel-anime characters against painterly environments.
- Uptown Galaxical Tower; Downtown CBD and hospital outside the WMD's sharply bounded annihilation field.
- Low-friction hobby: $0 incremental cost, proven technologies, small reviewable increments, no imposed video-generation pipeline.
- English and Traditional Chinese; accessibility, mobile/desktop, reversible local development.
