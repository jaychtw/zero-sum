## Applied editorial changes
1. **Two-vehicle pursuit:** Alex/Misaki and Jacque/Pete occupy distinct, synchronized vehicles at the opening. A brief emergency transfer bay allows Alex and Pete to switch cars if Alex chooses Karl; if Alex chooses the CBD, nobody switches. This makes both team assignments and Pete's later hospital role physically coherent.
2. **Pete:** His protection of a civilian motorist and radio reassurance of Misaki gives him a personality before either survival or death.
3. **Jacque:** Adjusted bridge dialogue toward urgency, retained his boldness; distinct final rescue-route cutaways show a living Jacque trying to contact Alex in E/F.
4. **Misaki and Alex:** More observable mutual concern while both remain competent; Misaki's conflicting professional and emotional motivations are shown rather than erased.
5. **Ending A versus D:** The grave scenes carry distinct emotional expression; A dwells on accountability for civilians, D on the cost of capturing Karl alive.
6. **Ending B versus C:** B emphasizes the futile ascent through the stairwell; C presents Alex directly confronting Karl's conviction. Karl's new line avoids implying literal resurrection.
7. **Ending H:** Includes two plausible, conflicting institutional judgments and career consequences without declaring one course objectively right.
8. **Ending I:** Weeks of isolation and self-erasure precede the unnamed vigilante; no triumphant moral reward for vengeance.
9. **Subtext over explanation:** Removed several explicit thematic narrator pronouncements. Where a viewer needs to infer Alex's uncertainty, staging carries the meaning.
10. **Action consistency:** Distinguishes Alex's jetpack from Jacque's damaged gear by their separate equipment; sphere remains opaque, featureless, sharp-edged and contained before Downtown.
