If the ramp-only region is too small or the roads do not connect, recompose the original single scene rather than paste a second view. If faces cannot also remain legible, loosen the crop or retain the current text-led panel. Do not invent numeric ROIs before source pixels exist.

Save every attempt as a separate lossless source and receipt, e.g. `fork-study-001.png`; never overwrite a source or call an unreviewed study a master. After a study, inspect all faces, seats, car bodies, road connectivity and crop pixels before one correction. No bulk variants, paid retry or silent provider change. Generating a candidate does not approve it. A composite board is rejected even if it contains two individually attractive views.
