## Reuse limits and production order

- Pursuit and transfer bay require two different masters: moving pursuit and a paused decision are spatially distinct. OPEN supports five panel slots, FORK four. A two-master pilot would therefore address nine current text-led panels, subject to readable cabin details. This is sequencing advice only; the owner must first choose a production method.
- Hanging Jacque, airborne Jacque and evacuated Jacque are different physical states. No crop can turn one into another. Tower access is architecture-only so death/rescue routes can share it; the stair boundary deliberately excludes Jacque.
- A shot-cost aftermath cannot reuse upright civilians as though they had fallen. A's protest cannot become D's calmer honors by hiding protesters. Their secondary couple detail can share a neutral edge composition.
- Ward reconciliation, clinical care, later emotional distance, solitary rooftop search and days-later rooftop intimacy have distinct emotional/time conditions. Hospital clinical/portrait details remove the handholding; they cannot change expression.
- Summit confrontation, summit erasure and city-wide boundary have distinct scale and timing. ERASURE/CITY are deferred faithful art specifications only, with no sphere experiment authorized by M5. The accepted left ascent crop continues to exclude the rejected ring.
- City boundary is a narrator insert shared by six panels, not six literal points of view. Grave sharing is restricted to A/D; E/F show Jacque alive in an open ambulance instead.
- Candidate existing crops are **not active**. Native subsets: Alex-alone 307×173, hangar landmark 461×259, restraint 742×418, nurse/IV clinical 320×180, Alex portrait 563×317. These are inset-size candidates, capped at native width in a future composition. Do not expand small crops to the desktop frame; loosen framing or keep the panel text-led if details fail. M5's [crop inspection sheet](../experiences/graphic-novel/docs/m5-evidence/candidate-crop-review.png) is a framing contact sheet, not new artwork or app appearance.
- Asset rights/provenance remain undocumented. Reuse recommendations do not confer production clearance. Do not retire source masters, crop destructively, or activate future art without source/rights records, bilingual alt/caption, native-size and mobile/desktop review.
