## S00 / ZS-S001 — Rain, pursuit

**EXT. MEGACITY EXPRESSWAYS — NIGHT**

Neon bleeds across the wet highway. The heavy traffic below continues its ordinary life, unaware of the convoy weaving above it. A black armored vehicle shoulders between two escort motorcycles. Beyond the rain, Galaxical Tower cuts a needle-shaped silhouette through Uptown's luminous haze.

Two pursuit cars thread through traffic, linked by a short-range squad channel. In the **lead car**, Alex drives while Misaki plots the exits from the passenger seat. In the **following car**, Jacque braces beside Pete, who is driving with one eye on fleeing civilian traffic. The four can see one another intermittently through the rain.

**PETE (radio):** Civilians on the eastbound lane. I'll draw the escorts off them.

Pete angles his car into the path of a cult motorcycle, opening a gap for a frightened driver to escape. Jacque fires from the moving passenger side, disabling the escort without hitting the civilian.

**JACQUE:** You always pick the difficult lane.

**PETE:** It had someone in it.

A cult escort swings across the lead lane. Alex corrects the wheel by inches. A round sparks off the barrier. Misaki puts her palm against the dash, then immediately resumes tracking the convoy.

**ALEX:** You all right?

**MISAKI:** Eyes ahead, commander. I've got the exits.

She gives him a quick look: the concern registers on both sides but neither has time to acknowledge it.

**ALEX:** Stay on the device. Don't get distracted by the escorts.

**MISAKI:** It's not one convoy anymore.

At the center interchange, the tail vehicles split. A group with heavier weapons turns toward the Downtown CBD. Karl's smaller convoy rises toward Uptown. The two exits diverge like an opening pair of scissors.

**CUT TO** Alex's hands on the wheel; the road sign's reflection moves across his eyes.

**CONTINUE → S01.**

## S01 / ZS-S002 — The Midtown junction

**INT. TWO PURSUIT VEHICLES / MIDTOWN INTERCHANGE — CONTINUOUS**

Maps flicker in both cars. Galaxical Tower rises above one branch of the highway; the Downtown CBD lies beyond the other. Two danger areas. One commander.

**MISAKI:** The Downtown group has the heavy weapons. Your rifle can stop them before they reach the square.

**JACQUE (radio):** Pete and I can take Karl. I've got the mobility.

**PETE (radio):** The signal's dropping. Whichever way we go, don't wait on the radio.

**ALEX:** And the device?

**MISAKI:** I know what's at stake. That's why I'm asking you to come with me.

An empty emergency transfer bay lies just before the fork. Both cars brake there for a few breaths. If Alex joins Misaki, he remains in the lead car and Jacque and Pete pursue Karl in the second. If he joins Jacque, **Alex and Pete exchange vehicles**: Pete takes the wheel beside Misaki toward Downtown; Alex joins Jacque in the other car toward Uptown. It is a rapid, visible transfer, not a mid-chase teleport.

Misaki and Alex meet eyes as the squad rearranges. Her recommendation is professional, not a plea for his companionship.

Both ramps are open. Neither will remain open.

**CHOICE D1 / ZS-S002**
- **Join Jacque: pursue Karl → S02**
- **Join Misaki: stop the assault team → S11**

# ROUTE J — Karl / Jacque

## S02 / ZS-S003 — Jetpack over the bridge

**EXT. UPTOWN EXPRESSWAY — NIGHT**

**If Alex chose Karl, the vehicle exchange at the transfer bay has already happened.** Pete is now driving with Misaki toward Downtown. Alex rides in the other pursuit car beside Jacque.

Alex switches their car into autopilot on a cleared stretch. Steering steadies within the lane. Both men fire through the rain, disabling Karl's escort formation while civilian cars pass outside their arcs.

**JACQUE:** You've got the road?

**ALEX:** The car has the road. I've got you.

Jacque smiles, snaps down his visor, and ignites his jetpack. He launches over the gap between vehicles, boots scraping against the hood of Karl's lead transport.

Karl turns. His shoulder-mounted mini-rocket system unfolds with an almost quiet mechanical click.

**ALEX:** Jacque—move!

Two bright streaks. The rockets break the bridge rail and throw Jacque sideways. He vanishes below Alex's sightline.

Alex stops hard. Beneath the fractured road, Jacque hangs by one gauntleted hand over the dark canyon between the towers. His jetpack spits and dies. Karl's taillights recede.

**JACQUE (radio, breathless):** Alex. I'm holding—but not for long.

The bridge shifts under his weight.

**CHOICE D2-J / ZS-S003**
- **Continue after Karl → S03**
- **Turn back and save Jacque → S08**

## S03 / ZS-S004 — The cost of distance

**EXT. UPTOWN ROAD / GALAXICAL APPROACH — NIGHT**

Alex accelerates. He cannot look back again. In the mirror: a hand on broken metal, then nothing. Jacque slips from the bridge into darkness. The radio emits only static.

A final escort turns to block the passage. Alex fires from the moving vehicle, bringing it to a halt across an empty service lane. For a moment Karl's car is directly ahead.

The radio clears.

**PETE (radio):** Alex, we took an RPG. Misaki's hurt—badly. I'm getting her to the Downtown hospital.

Alex's eyes flash toward the speaker.

**ALEX:** Is she conscious?

**PETE:** She's talking. I'm taking her in. Don't divide yourself again for us.

Pete's voice steadies despite the urgency. Through the open channel Alex hears him tell Misaki, gently but firmly, to keep pressure on the wound.

By the time Alex looks up, Karl's driver has stopped by the tower hangar. Karl is already running toward the interior. The device bag knocks against his leg.

Alex sees a possible line of fire. He also sees the vehicle's momentum and the hangar opening.

**CHOICE D3-J / ZS-S004**
- **Take the anti-materiel rifle → S04**
- **Drive straight at Karl → ENDING D**

## S04 / ZS-S005 — A line through strangers

**EXT. GALAXICAL TOWER HANGAR APPROACH — NIGHT**

Alex unfolds the long anti-materiel rifle and settles it against a damaged parapet. Through the sight, Karl is sharp and unhurried, framed by the hangar's service lights.

Then two civilians emerge from a corridor behind Karl. One pulls the other through the stream of departing workers. Their movement crosses the same line as Karl's chest.

Alex knows the round can pass through its first target. A clean strike is not a safe strike.

**ALEX (under his breath):** Move. Please move.

Karl reaches the threshold.

There is no warning chime and no tactical recommendation. Only the weight of a trigger beneath Alex's finger.

**CHOICE D4-J / ZS-S005**
- **Fire now → ENDING A**
- **Wait for a clean shot → S05**

## S05 / ZS-S006 — The tower is closed

**EXT. / INT. GALAXICAL TOWER ENTRANCE — NIGHT**

Alex holds his shot. The civilians clear the lane—too late. Karl disappears beyond an armored hangar door. Alex reaches the entrance as the door seals behind him.

Inside, elevators stand dark. Open panels expose cleanly cut wiring. The stairwell climbs beyond the illuminated floors. Outside, service gantries run upward into rain and cloud.

**ALEX (radio):** Karl's inside. Elevators sabotaged. I'm going up.

No answer from Jacque.

Alex checks the stairs, then the jetpack mounted on his own kit. Neither path offers certainty.

**CHOICE D5-J / ZS-S006**
- **Take the stairs → ENDING B**
- **Ascend outside by jetpack → ENDING C**

## ENDING A / ZS-S011 — Cost of Victory

**EXT. HANGAR APPROACH — CONTINUOUS**

Alex fires. The sound has no echo in the moment, only impact. Karl collapses. The device bag skids away, unopened.

The two civilians behind him fall.

**HOLD** on Alex's eye through the sight. He lowers the rifle; his finger stays curled for a moment after it has left the trigger. Far below, a responder rushes toward the two fallen civilians.

**EXT. CIVIC SQUARE — DAYS LATER**

A city official places a medal around Alex's neck. Behind the crowd, screens announce that Uptown was saved. Other screens show the wounded CBD, the dead from the assault, and the two civilians caught by Alex's shot.

**MAYOR:** For preventing a catastrophe—

The rest is drowned by shouting. Protest signs rise. Someone throws rubbish toward the steps. Alex does not retaliate.

At the far edge of the gathering, Pete helps Misaki through the crowd. She rests her head briefly against his shoulder. Their tenderness is unmistakable, and it is not directed at Alex.

**EXT. JACQUE'S GRAVE — DUSK**

Rain gathers in the lettering. Alex kneels, unfastens the medal, and lays it against the stone.

**ALEX:** They put your name on the report.

He turns the medal over. Its polished face reflects neither the crowd nor the grave for very long.

He walks away without it.

**END A.**

## ENDING B / ZS-S012 — The stairwell

**INT. GALAXICAL TOWER STAIRWELL — NIGHT**

Alex climbs past abandoned offices and shuttered safety doors. The emergency lights disappear one landing at a time.

**ALEX (radio):** Karl, if you can hear me, stop the sequence.

No response. Above, a white brilliance spills through the seams of a door. Alex runs faster.

The illumination becomes a perfect white boundary, not flame. Beyond it, the handrail, stair, and wall cease to exist. Alex is still moving when the advancing edge reaches him.

**EXT. CITY — WIDE**

A sphere of absolute white expands around Galaxical Tower, erasing the matter within it. It consumes Uptown. It stops before Downtown; the hospital and CBD remain beyond its sharp edge.

A red light blinks on Alex's abandoned radio channel. Then it, too, disappears.

**END B.**

## ENDING C / ZS-S013 — The last ascent

**EXT. GALAXICAL TOWER EXTERIOR — NIGHT**

Jetpack fire briefly outshines the rain. Alex ascends along the tower's black glass, passes maintenance decks and dead elevators, and clears the highest obstruction.

Above, Karl works beside an opened portable device at the summit. The space around it is not a portal. Its activation has not yet produced the white field.

Alex comes into view.

**ALEX:** Karl! Step away from it!

Karl looks up. He sees the jetpack, the rifle, and the man who is still trying to stop him. His strange smile is almost relieved.

**KARL:** You came all this way to save a city.

**ALEX:** Step away. There are people below us.

Karl glances over the towers, then back at Alex. He does not retreat.

**KARL:** I won't see what comes next. They will remember why it began.

Karl activates the device.

**BEAT ONE — THE LAST APPROACH:** Alex cuts power to descend toward the summit. Karl's hand rests on the device. We remain near Alex long enough to see he has not given up.

**BEAT TWO — ACTIVATION:** A featureless white spherical field forms around the device and expands with an unnaturally clean edge. No ring, flame, transparent center, or conventional blast. Alex reaches forward; the summit and Karl are silently erased.

**BEAT THREE — THE BOUNDARY:** His jetpack exhaust goes out against the advancing white edge. From surviving Downtown, the field is now a sharply bounded volume where Uptown once stood. It grows no farther. The CBD and hospital remain intact beyond it.

Neither man outlives the event. Nothing suggests Karl's body will return.

**END C.**

## ENDING D / ZS-S014 — Victory Through Sacrifice

**EXT. GALAXICAL TOWER HANGAR — NIGHT**

Alex accelerates straight toward Karl. Karl looks back, startled, and darts into a corner near the hangar wall. He drops to one knee and opens the WMD bag.

The car is still moving when Alex releases the restraints and jumps.

His shoulder drives into Karl before the activation can complete. Both hit the concrete. The device slides across the floor. Alex pins Karl until backup storms the hangar.

**OFFICER:** Device secure! Suspect alive!

**EXT. CIVIC SQUARE — DAYS LATER**

Uptown remains. Downtown bears scars from the assault team's attack. Alex is commended before cameras and families who know saving one part of the city did not erase losses elsewhere.

Pete and Misaki stand close together. They have become lovers. Alex sees them and offers a small, sincere nod.

**EXT. JACQUE'S GRAVE — DUSK**

Alex sets the award on the headstone.

**ALEX:** I wish you could've seen it.

The grave is quiet. He places the award flat on the stone rather than hanging it there, and sits beside it for a long while.

**END D.**

## S08 / ZS-S007 — Return for Jacque

**EXT. BROKEN BRIDGE — NIGHT**

Alex abandons the immediate chase and races back across the damaged span. He crawls onto the fractured edge, catches Jacque's wrist, and hauls him onto solid pavement.

Jacque tries to rise and cannot. His jetpack is crushed.

**JACQUE:** Karl—

**ALEX:** I know.

The skyline swallows Karl's disappearing convoy.

Pete's voice breaks through the radio: the Downtown vehicle has been hit, and he is taking wounded Misaki to hospital.

By the time Alex reaches Galaxical Tower with Jacque, Karl is inside and the elevators are disabled. Backup arrives to evacuate the injured man.

**JACQUE:** Don't carry me up there too.

**ALEX:** Stay alive. That's an order you can follow.

Alex faces the interior stairs and the rain-swept exterior.

**CHOICE D3-R / ZS-S007**
- **Take the stairs → ENDING E**
- **Use the exterior jetpack → ENDING F**

## ENDING E / ZS-S015 — The rescued

**INT. GALAXICAL TOWER STAIRWELL — NIGHT**

Jacque is safely in backup's care. Alex runs upward alone. The tower shudders. A perfect line of white reaches into the stairwell and consumes the railing, walls, and Alex before he can reach Karl.

**EXT. DOWNTOWN — WIDE**

The white sphere erases Uptown and stops before Downtown.

**EXT. EMERGENCY EXTRACTION BAY — SAME NIGHT**

Jacque, bandaged and breathing, struggles upright on a stretcher as the sky whitens. A medic keeps him from stepping into the street. His damaged jetpack lies beside the ambulance, still smelling faintly of fuel.

**JACQUE:** Alex? Answer me.

Only static.

**END E.**

## ENDING F / ZS-S016 — The exterior after rescue

**EXT. GALAXICAL TOWER — NIGHT**

With Jacque evacuated, Alex climbs along the outer glass by jetpack. Karl stands at the top, hands near the activated device. He looks upward and smiles, untroubled by the prospect of his own death.

Alex reaches for his rifle. The advancing white boundary overtakes Karl, then the jetpack's blue exhaust, then Alex himself.

**EXT. DOWNTOWN EVACUATION BAY — SAME NIGHT**

From his evacuation vehicle, Jacque sees the white sphere consume the tower's silhouette. He knows Alex took the exterior route; he cannot see him anymore. He reaches for the radio with a hand that will not stop trembling.

**JACQUE:** Come on. Just answer.

No signal. The sphere stops before Downtown.

**END F.**

# ROUTE M — Misaki / Downtown

## S11 / ZS-S008 — The weapon that isn't stopped

**EXT. DOWNTOWN APPROACH — NIGHT**

Alex and Misaki move through the cult convoy's heavier vehicles. Alex takes a narrow shot through an opening between civilian cars. Misaki anticipates the next enemy turn before it happens. They work as equals.

**MISAKI:** Left. The launcher vehicle's trying to get above us.

**ALEX:** I see him.

A burst over the radio, then Jacque.

**JACQUE (radio):** Alex... Pete's down. He's gone. We were still on Karl.

For half a second the vehicle holds a straight line.

**ALEX:** Jacque, confirm—

A rocket strikes. Alex reacts on instinct, throwing up the vehicle's shield. The blast breaks around his side; Misaki is thrown hard against the damaged interior.

The vehicle skids to a stop. Blood runs under Misaki's armor. She is conscious, but in pain. She reaches for her firearm.

**MISAKI:** Go. They're almost at the square.

Alex supports her shoulders, careful not to move the injured side. He searches her eyes before answering.

**ALEX:** Stay with me long enough to get help.

**MISAKI:** I'm not going anywhere. You need to.

Her grip tightens on the weapon. She genuinely expects him to keep fighting. Her eyes, however, remain on his face longer than necessary.

**CHOICE D2-M / ZS-S008**
- **Take Misaki to hospital yourself → S12**
- **Request backup for Misaki and pursue the attackers → S13**

## S12 / ZS-S009 — The ward

**INT. DOWNTOWN HOSPITAL — NIGHT**

The emergency doors close behind Alex's shoulders. Misaki is carried into a ward. Even with a medic supporting her, she tries to sit up and return to the mission.

**MISAKI:** You heard the radio. They're still in the CBD.

**PHYSICIAN:** And you won't be, unless you let us treat you.

**ALEX:** Listen to the doctor.

**MISAKI:** That's easy for you to say.

She looks at him. The anger has nowhere firm to settle: he brought her here, and she is grateful.

Outside the hospital window, Galaxical Tower is a distant thread of light. Alex's radio can no longer hold a steady signal from Jacque.

**ALEX:** I might get him from the roof.

Misaki turns her face away before replying.

**MISAKI:** Do what you think you have to.

**CHOICE D3-M / ZS-S009**
- **Remain at Misaki's bedside → ENDING G**
- **Go to the roof to contact Jacque → ENDING H**

## ENDING G / ZS-S017 — Love Amid the Ruins

**INT. DOWNTOWN HOSPITAL WARD — NIGHT**

Alex remains. At first neither of them speaks. Then Misaki reaches across the narrow space between bed and chair. Alex takes her hand.

**MISAKI:** I meant it when I told you to go.

**ALEX:** I know.

**MISAKI:** I meant something else, too.

He doesn't ask her to explain. Instead he shifts the chair closer so she can take his hand without reaching.

The room fills with white light. Beyond the glass, a perfect expanding sphere engulfs Uptown. It does not cross into Downtown. No fireball reaches the hospital. The missing part of the skyline is revealed when the brightness subsides.

Misaki's hand tightens.

**MISAKI:** Jacque?

Alex doesn't answer. There is no answer he can truthfully give.

**EXT. DOWNTOWN ROOFTOP — DAYS LATER**

The city continues in the shadow of its vanished district. Alex and Misaki stand together as a couple. She leans against him. Their closeness is real, but Alex's eyes return to the empty horizon.

**ALEX:** The empty part never looks the same twice.

Misaki follows his gaze to the absent skyline. After a moment she rests her hand over his.

**MISAKI:** No. It doesn't.

They remain together, unable to undo what happened.

**END G.**

## ENDING H / ZS-S018 — Weight of Command

**INT. HOSPITAL WARD / EXT. ROOFTOP — NIGHT**

Alex rises.

**ALEX:** I'll be back when I've reached him.

Misaki nods. Her face closes a little before he turns away.

The rooftop is cold and loud with distant sirens. Alex raises the radio above the parapet and searches through static.

**ALEX:** Jacque. Respond. Jacque!

**JACQUE (radio, faint):** Alex... I'm sorry. I couldn't stop him.

**ALEX:** Where are you?

The signal tears apart. Across the city, white light unfolds into an impossibly clean sphere around Galaxical Tower. Uptown disappears within it; the sphere stops short of Downtown.

**INT. INQUIRY CHAMBER — WEEKS LATER**

A wall displays two maps: the Downtown gunmen's line of fire and the destroyed Uptown. Testimony plays in fragments.

**INQUIRY OFFICER:** You chose to bring an injured operative to safety.

**SECOND OFFICER:** And after that, you left the ward to establish contact. Why not return to the field?

**ALEX:** The routes were closing. I didn't know where Karl was.

An official notes that evacuating Misaki was consistent with duty of care. Another asks why an officer of Alex's capability was not at the tower. No one can prove their preferred alternative would have stopped the device; the uncertainty does not protect him from the political demand for an answer.

News feeds reduce the proceedings to a single question: **WHERE WAS THE COMMANDER?**

**EXT. SERVICE ENTRANCE — LATER**

Alex exits carrying a small box of command credentials. His dismissal papers are folded on top. He does not look toward the cameras.

**EXT. HOSPITAL EXIT — LATER**

Misaki recovers enough to walk outside. Alex approaches, but neither knows how to close the distance the rooftop door began.

**MISAKI:** Are you all right?

**ALEX:** I don't know.

She has no reply that can repair it.

**END H.**

## S13 / ZS-S010 — The square

**EXT. DOWNTOWN CBD — NIGHT**

Alex radios for medical extraction. Misaki resists the stretcher until the backup leader promises to keep the pursuit moving.

**MISAKI:** They're almost there. You know what to do.

**ALEX:** Backup has you.

**MISAKI:** Go.

Her voice is firm; her gaze carries a separate hurt. Alex does not see her again before turning toward the CBD.

On the radio Jacque reports that Karl's convoy is slowing strangely.

**JACQUE (radio):** They're losing speed. I don't like it.

**ALEX:** Stay on him. You've got this.

The signal ends.

In the square, the cult assault team fires into people fleeing among vehicles and storefronts. Alex takes a protected vantage. He cannot save everyone. He can stop the shooters in his sightline.

One controlled anti-materiel shot. Reposition. Another. The last gunman collapses behind a concrete kiosk. Silence is broken by cries for help, not celebration.

Then white light floods the CBD sky.

**CONTINUE → ENDING I.**

## ENDING I / ZS-S019 — The Unnamed

**EXT. CBD SQUARE — CONTINUOUS**

Alex looks toward Uptown. A sharply bounded white sphere consumes the distant district but stops before the CBD. His rifle hangs heavy in his hands. The people immediately around him live; millions farther away do not.

**EXT. DOWNTOWN — DAYS, THEN WEEKS LATER**

Jacque remains missing, presumed dead. The CBD survivors recognize Alex and offer gratitude that he cannot comfortably accept. He returns to the square once, then again; candles accumulate where the attackers stood.

Misaki recovers but answers his calls less often. Their next conversation takes place beneath an awning outside a rehabilitation clinic. She speaks with the controlled distance of someone who has been hurt and cannot simply reverse it.

**MISAKI:** You did what you came to do.

**ALEX:** That isn't the same as enough.

**MISAKI:** I didn't say it was.

**INT. ALEX'S APARTMENT — NIGHT, WEEKS LATER**

Photographs of the squad remain on the table. Alex has covered the city inquiry notices but not thrown them away. On a scrap map, he circles known locations of surviving cultists. He hears a message from a grateful CBD survivor; he does not play it a second time.

He removes his squad insignia and puts it in the drawer.

**EXT. RAIN-SOAKED ALLEY — NIGHT, LATER**

A cult remnant runs beneath a flickering sign. A figure emerges from the shadow before him. He wears no public commendation and gives no name.

Only when the figure turns slightly do we recognize Alex. The cultist turns a corner; Alex follows without triumph or music that promises justice.

**HOLD** on the rain washing down an empty alley. What this pursuit will make of him remains unanswered.

**END I.**
