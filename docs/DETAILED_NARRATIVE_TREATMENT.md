## World and invariant rules

- The story is a futuristic retro-anime cyberpunk action thriller in an enormous, rain-slick, neon megacity. Its shared visual style is expressive crisp 1980s–1990s cel characters in painterly cinematic environments.
- The squad: **Alex** (leader, extraordinary marksman, moral restraint, feelings for Misaki), **Misaki** (tactically incisive and independent, emotionally complex), **Jacque** (brave, strong close-range fighter and jetpack user), **Pete** (trustworthy, caring comrade with feelings for Misaki).
- **Karl** is an ideologically driven cult leader carrying an exotic portable WMD in a bag/device assembly. He is willing to perish; his imagined 'rebirth' is the persistence of his *cause*, not literal bodily survival. He can fire miniature rockets from a shoulder-mounted system.
- **Galaxical Tower** is in **Uptown**. The **CBD and hospital** are **Downtown**. Pursuit divides at a **Midtown highway junction**.
- The device produces an expanding, opaque, featureless **white spherical field with a sharp boundary**. Matter and people within it cease to exist. Its growth stops **before Downtown**. Do not depict it as a portal/ring, transparent shield, ordinary explosion, uncontrolled shockwave or burning fireball. Precise radius remains undefined.
- A second cult assault team attacks the CBD and may cause hundreds of deaths even when Karl is stopped in Uptown. Saving Uptown does not imply total prevention of casualties.
- Alex lives **one** timeline and has no knowledge of alternatives. All endings A–I are legitimate; none is declared best or morally rewarded. Rational or principled decisions can end disastrously. The viewer may replay to see alternatives without imposing that omniscience on Alex.

## Characters: personality in action, not route-dependent rewrites

**Alex:** decisive, restrained, resourceful commander, especially lethal when using the anti-materiel rifle. His dilemmas test which legitimate duty he can serve and what risk he permits himself to impose on others. On some routes he sacrifices loyalty, on others opportunities for love, on others a chance to stop the WMD. Catastrophic outcomes do not automatically imply poor character or poor reasoning.

**Misaki:** independent, tough, assertive and strategically persuasive. At the split she herself advises Alex to accompany her because his superior shooting can counter the assault team's heavy weapons; she trusts Jacque and Pete to handle Karl. When wounded she still reaches for her weapon and urges him to continue, professionally recognizing the mission—yet personally feels hurt or abandoned when he does. Both sides are sincere.

**Jacque:** confident powerhouse with jetpack and a bold willingness to take physical risks. His attempt to close with Karl exposes a vulnerability; Alex may lose or save him. In the hospital-rooftop route his final radio call is apologetic, not cowardly.

**Pete:** compassionate, dependable teammate, not solely a romantic obstacle. In the Karl-pursuit branch he evacuates Misaki to hospital and they eventually become partners; in the Misaki/CBD branch Pete is reported killed while pursuing Karl with Jacque. His specialized field role was not definitively finalized, so do not invent one as settled.

**Karl:** persuasive and terrifyingly certain of the righteousness of annihilation. His calm smile beside the activated WMD expresses ideological conviction rather than survival. A previously proposed *candidate* line was: “You think I'm doing this for myself? I'll perish with the old world! But my cause will be reborn in the new order!” This is NOT a confirmed locked verbatim screenplay line.

## Shared opening — S00: The pursuit and the decisive fork

A high-speed confrontation through a densely lit cyberpunk city. Cars and motorcycles, gunfire, wet streets, escort vehicles and tactical radio chatter. Karl transports the WMD toward Galaxical Tower; an armed cult assault team separates toward the CBD. At the Midtown junction Misaki recommends Alex join her to confront the heavy weapons in Downtown because he is the best marksman, leaving Jacque and Pete to continue after Karl. Alex chooses:

- **Junction / D1-A:** Join **Jacque** against **Karl**, leaving **Pete and Misaki** to counter the assault force.
- **Junction / D1-B:** Join **Misaki** against the CBD **assault team**, leaving **Jacque and Pete** after Karl.

The decision is genuinely defensible in either direction and not simply a romance choice.

## Path A — Alex accompanies Jacque against Karl

### A1: Escort fight and Jacque hanging

Alex switches the vehicle to autopilot so both he and Jacque can fire on Karl's escort. Jacque activates his jetpack and lunges toward Karl. Karl fires miniature shoulder rockets; Jacque is blasted onto the edge of a high, broken bridge and is left clinging above a fatal fall.

**D2-A:** (1) **Continue the pursuit** or (2) **Save Jacque**.

### A2-A: Leave Jacque and continue

Jacque slips and falls to his death. Alex continues driving, eliminating additional escorts. Pete reports by radio that an RPG struck their vehicle; Misaki is seriously injured and Pete is taking her to a **Downtown hospital**. A brief disruption of Alex's concentration allows Karl to escape the vehicle and reach the Galaxical Tower hangar.

**D3-A:** (1) **Use the penetrative sniper rifle** or (2) **intercept/tackle Karl with the moving vehicle**.

### A3-A: Sniper dilemma

Alex has Karl in his anti-materiel rifle's sights. Civilians move through or behind his firing lane. The rifle overpenetrates; even a perfect hit on Karl risks killing innocents beyond him.

**D4:** (1) **Fire despite the civilians** or (2) **Wait for a clean shot**.

#### Ending A — Cost of Victory (shoot)

Alex fires, kills Karl, and kills **two civilians** behind the target. The Uptown annihilation does not occur; however, the separate CBD assault caused serious casualties. Jacque is dead. In the aftermath, Pete and Misaki have become lovers. The mayor awards Alex a medal while protesters condemn the civilian deaths and throw refuse at him. At Jacque's grave, Alex leaves the medal on the headstone. Victory is neither pure nor uncomplicated.

### A4-B: Hold the shot

Alex declines the potentially lethal collateral shot. Karl makes it into Galaxical Tower; elevators have been sabotaged.

**D5:** (1) Take the **stairs** or (2) ascend the tower **exterior by jetpack**.

#### Ending B — Stairs, too late

Alex races up the tower's interior staircase. Halfway up, the expanding white matter-annihilation sphere engulfs him and erases much of Uptown. It halts before Downtown.

#### Ending C — Exterior ascent, too late

Alex uses the jetpack up the outside of the tower. Near its summit, he sees Karl operating the extraordinary device. Karl glances upward and gives a strange, serene smile, fully accepting that he will die with the old order; he believes the cause will survive. The sharp-edged white spherical field bursts outward, erasing Karl, Alex and Uptown within its boundary; Downtown survives. The precise event is **activation → opaque sphere expands → matter inside disappears → boundary stops short of Downtown**, not a portal or ring.

### A3-B: Vehicle tackle

#### Ending D — Victory Through Sacrifice

Instead of taking the sniper approach, Alex drives directly at Karl. Startled, Karl runs toward the hangar corner and opens his WMD bag. Alex leaps from the moving vehicle and tackles Karl unconscious before he can activate the weapon. Backup arrives and arrests Karl. Uptown is spared the erasure, though the CBD assault still caused suffering. Jacque died; Pete and Misaki are lovers. Alex receives formal recognition but leaves the medal at Jacque's grave.

### A2-B: Rescue Jacque instead

Alex pulls the injured Jacque to safety; Karl disappears. Pete radios that Misaki was wounded and is being taken to hospital. At the tower, backup evacuates Jacque while Alex finds the elevators sabotaged.

**D3-B:** (1) Stairs or (2) jetpack exterior.

#### Ending E — Rescue, interior ascent

Jacque survives because Alex returned. Alex races up the tower stairs but is consumed by Karl's expanding white sphere before reaching him. Uptown is erased, Downtown remains.

#### Ending F — Rescue, exterior ascent

Jacque survives; Alex climbs outside by jetpack. Alex glimpses Karl's unsettling final smile near the device; the white matter-annihilation sphere consumes them and Uptown, but stops before Downtown.

## Path B — Alex accompanies Misaki against the CBD assault

### B1: Heavy-weapons pursuit and Misaki's injury

Alex and Misaki skillfully pursue and neutralize cultists. Jacque radios that Pete was shot and killed while they chased Karl. Suddenly an RPG strikes Alex and Misaki's vehicle. Alex instinctively activates a shield that protects him, but Misaki suffers serious, **nonfatal** injuries. Even wounded she reaches for her gun, urges Alex to keep going and insists she can manage. Internally, she does not entirely want to be left alone. This contradiction is central to her character, not an inconsistency.

**D2-B:** (1) **Take Misaki to hospital** or (2) **Call backup for Misaki and continue after the assault team**.

### B2-A: Accompany Misaki to the Downtown hospital

Misaki appreciates that Alex remains. Meanwhile the cult assault team continues toward the CBD; chaos worsens. Despite her injuries Misaki tries to rise and return to duty at the hospital, but the physician and Alex insist on treatment.

**D3-C:** (1) **Stay at Misaki's bedside** or (2) **Leave for the hospital rooftop to contact Jacque**.

#### Ending G — Love Amid the Ruins

Alex stays beside Misaki; they share a tender handholding moment. From the Downtown hospital they witness the enormous but bounded white erasure of Uptown, safely outside its radius. Both survive, but millions are gone and Jacque is presumed dead. In the following days Alex and Misaki become lovers. Their relationship contains genuine affection but is shadowed by Alex's guilt and the skyline's permanent absence.

#### Ending H — Weight of Command

Misaki is visibly disappointed when Alex leaves her room, even though he seeks contact with the missing squad. From the hospital roof he obtains better radio reception. Jacque apologizes that he could not stop Karl. Alex watches the white spherical annihilation erase Uptown and stop before Downtown. Alex survives, but investigation, blame and public scrutiny destroy his career and reputation. Misaki survives; their relationship remains unresolved.

### B2-B: Leave Misaki with backup; pursue the attackers

Misaki agrees reluctantly for professional reasons while feeling personally abandoned. Alex calls Jacque: Jacque remains after Karl, but reports the convoy is strangely slowing; the call cuts short. Alex trusts him and continues. In the Downtown CBD public square the assault team is killing civilians. Alex takes cover and uses his anti-materiel rifle to methodically eliminate the attackers, stopping the massacre. Suddenly an overwhelming white light illuminates the skyline: the expanding, sharply bounded annihilation sphere has erased Uptown but spared Downtown.

#### Ending I — The Unnamed

In the aftermath Jacque is lost/presumed dead and Misaki becomes emotionally distant. Alex is haunted by the failure to prevent the city-scale atrocity despite having saved people at the CBD square. Days later he abandons his former identity and becomes an anonymous, rain-soaked vigilante hunting the surviving cult members. This is a transformation through grief and bitterness, not a secret 'best' ending.

## Narrative production policy

1. Every route should be dramatically self-sufficient; do not force nine equivalent-length endings or arbitrary five-second blocks.
2. The nine endings are **not scores or ethical verdicts**. In B/C Alex demonstrates deep moral restraint by refusing to risk civilians, despite catastrophic consequence.
3. No one acquires counterfactual information. Only the audience's replay reveals possible alternatives.
4. A choice may be thoughtful, brave, ethical or skillful and still produce harm; the film should not rewrite earlier information to blame Alex retrospectively.
5. Visual work may use **interactive graphic novel, visual novel, motion comic, hybrid interactive anime, or later action game**—one shared canon, multiple expressions. Do not require mastery of a burdensome video pipeline.
6. Keep localized EN/zh-TW presentation. This treatment is **English-only narrative source**; the bilingual script itself is still to be authored/reconciled.
7. Do not treat existing 19 runtime nodes as a complete shot breakdown. They are condensed presentation data with stable mappings in the narrative registry.
