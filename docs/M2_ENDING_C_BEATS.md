## Rejected sphere study

A $0 CSS experiment used the existing city crop behind an opaque white disk, progressing from 43% to 92% of frame width. It preserved a circular silhouette, erased all detail inside, and used no ring, border glow, fireball or debris.

[Rejected browser capture](../experiences/graphic-novel/docs/m2-evidence/rejected-sphere-study.jpg).

**Rejected for composition, not promoted as faithful artwork:** the cropped city plate still contains the convoy and firing figures; the white disk reads as pasted over the illustration rather than an integrated annihilation volume. Further enlargement would magnify the low-resolution crop and would not fix perspective/geography. It does not convincingly establish Downtown outside the field. No attempt was made to disguise those issues with animation or effects. The rejected disk code is removed from the runtime; the screenshot is retained solely as evidence of the bounded experiment.

## Implemented interim alternative and precise art brief

Keep the existing Alex ascent close-up, then use two restrained, explicitly labeled **illustration-pending** cards with accurate bilingual narration. This strengthens dramatic pacing and removes the false portal depiction from Ending C, but **does not complete the faithful sphere-art criterion**. Other M1 endings are deliberately untouched and still have their documented provisional art.

For the next art step, the smallest useful input is **one clean, approved city panorama**, not a character/video asset campaign:

- A wide view from the surviving Downtown side toward Uptown's Galaxical Tower, with foreground Downtown architecture and a clear spatial gap to the tower. No foreground combat convoy or portal machinery; no new character designs needed.
- One opaque, featureless white spherical volume centered at the tower, with a crisp projected circular boundary. It occludes/erases all matter inside, including tower detail. No transparent center, luminous outline, concentric rings, fire, shockwave or debris cloud.
- A final still shows the field stopped short of Downtown, with intact foreground city detail visibly outside. Do not label a distance or invent a canonical radius. The same plate and a second mask can supply the activation and final panels; no animation is required.
- Compose for 16:9 and verify the full plate at 390px before scaling. Preserve the existing cel-anime/painterly palette and illustration integration. Asset source/rights need confirmation before production promotion.

A clean plate must be supplied or deliberately authored and reviewed before attempting another integrated sphere panel. This cycle does not introduce a new provider, generation workflow, paid service, or art-production pipeline.
