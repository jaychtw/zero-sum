# Zero Sum — Creative Canon Summary (2026-11-05)

This is a compact orientation, not a replacement for the conversation's fully drafted script. The authoritative narrative must be preserved and imported in full before any final adaptation is approved.

## Premise
An elite four-person team led by Alex pursues cult leader Karl, who aims to activate a portable annihilation-field device at Galaxical Tower in Uptown. At a junction, Karl's group separates from an assault team headed toward the Downtown CBD. Alex must choose whether to join Jacque against Karl or Misaki against the assault team. Pete is the fourth teammate.

## Character anchors
- **Alex**: exceptional marksman and capable commander; moral restraint; finite reach; cannot know roads not taken.
- **Misaki**: assertive, tactically sound, independent operative who recommends Alex counter the CBD team's firepower; urges him to continue even when injured, but experiences genuine emotional hurt if left behind.
- **Jacque**: loyal, confident close-combat powerhouse with jetpack; courageous and sometimes overconfident.
- **Pete**: grounded, caring comrade who also loves Misaki. Can die pursuing Karl or survive and become Misaki's partner.
- **Karl**: fanatical ideological revolutionary willing to die; expects his beliefs, not his body, to endure. Shoulder-mounted mini-rockets.

## World rules
Uptown contains Galaxical Tower. Downtown contains the CBD and hospital. Karl's WMD creates a sharply bounded expanding white sphere that erases matter inside, but does not reach Downtown. Radius intentionally unspecified; do not substitute an uncontrolled citywide blast.

## Branches and outcomes
1. **A** pursue Karl with Jacque. When Jacque falls from a bridge:
   - Leave Jacque; later choose sniper or vehicle tackle.
   - Sniper: fire through civilians => **Ending A**: Karl killed, city saved, two civilians killed, Jacque dead; public honors and protest, medal on grave.
   - Sniper: wait => Karl escapes into tower; stairs => **B**, jetpack => **C**, Alex dies in Uptown annihilation.
   - Vehicle tackle => **D**: Karl captured, city saved; Jacque dead, Pete and Misaki become lovers; medal on grave.
   - Save Jacque => Karl escapes; stairs => **E**, jetpack => **F**; Alex dies in Uptown annihilation. Jacque rescued earlier.
2. **B** pursue CBD assault team with Misaki; Pete dies pursuing Karl; Misaki injured.
   - Stay with Misaki and go to hospital; stay in ward => **G**, Alex and Misaki survive devastation in Downtown and later find love amid guilt.
   - Go to rooftop to contact Jacque => **H**, Alex survives, hears Jacque's apology, witnesses annihilation, career ruined and relationship uncertain.
   - Call backup for Misaki, continue pursuit => **I**, Alex snipes CBD attackers, watches Uptown erased, later becomes an unnamed vigilante against surviving cult members; Misaki emotionally distant.

## Moral architecture
No canonical best life. Every choice sacrifices possibilities. A sound decision does not ensure success; do not judge moral worth merely by outcome. Each branch should stand on its own dramatically. The audience may see many timelines, but Alex lives only one.

## Formats
One shared canon, progressively richer presentation: graphic novel → visual novel → motion comic → hybrid anime; potential action game. No requirement to complete every format or learn unfamiliar AI-video production.
